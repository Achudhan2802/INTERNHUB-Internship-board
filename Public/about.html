OUR MISSION

Making internship discovery simpler

InternHub is a fictional student internship platform designed to make it easier for students to discover opportunities that match their interests and skills.

Instead of searching through scattered websites, students can explore internship opportunities, filter them by domain and work mode, view detailed information, and submit applications from one place.
